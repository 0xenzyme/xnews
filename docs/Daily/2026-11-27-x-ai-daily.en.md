## 1. AI & science

### A 4D quantum-gravity preprint co-developed with GPT-6 Astra: finite amplitudes, black-hole entropy recovered

- What happened: Chandrasekaran posted "An algebraic quantization of gravity from the null cone" (arXiv:2611.34924, affiliation Stanford's Leinweber Institute for Theoretical Physics). It proposes a background-independent quantization of vacuum general relativity in 4D Lorentzian spacetime by solving the gravitational constraints on intersecting null cones before quantizing. Explainer threads say it yields finite nonperturbative graviton pair-creation amplitudes, a finite-dimensional Hilbert space for bounded regions, and the Bekenstein-Hawking entropy; the author says several calculations and proofs came from collaborating with GPT-6 Astra (~400M tokens). Sam Altman called Ven "one of the earliest believers that we could use our then-current model to solve millennium prize problems."
- Why it matters: After the 9/4 matrix-multiplication result and the Riemann proof, "AI does research" reaches theoretical physics. The difference: a human physicist led and the model was a long-running collaborator. Physicist Garrett Lisi's first read: "the work seems mostly Ven's... with extensive help from Astra."

- Verification: preprint and abstract verified (arXiv page readable); "~400M tokens" and "proofs co-developed with Astra" are author-claim (relayed via reposts; we did not read the acknowledgements); physics not peer reviewed, unverified. Ven's exact relationship to OpenAI rests only on Altman's post.
- Take: The story is the human-led, model-as-long-term-collaborator workflow, not "AI solved quantum gravity." Wait for expert reviews.

### Tencent open-sources DFly2: a speculative drafter that reads the target model's KV cache

- What happened: Tencent Hunyuan open-sourced DFly2, the drafter behind Hy4 flash. Draft layers read the target model's KV cache and top-k tokens directly, with no prefill and zero extra KV memory, so drafting cost stays flat at any context length. Tencent reports up to 81% higher DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash serving throughput at 64K context, up to 4.2× on Hy4 flash agentic tasks, and ~30% over same-recipe DSpark.
- Why it matters: Decoding is the bottleneck for long-context agents. DFly2 works on third-party sparse models (DeepSeek, GLM-5.3-Flash); a DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash drafter is already on HF, and community ports for DGX Spark and AMD are appearing on GitHub.
- Source & engagement: [@TencentAI_News](https://x.com/TencentAI_News/status/2125968859450802415) | ~90K views, 1,426 likes, 847 bookmarks; [@TencentHunyuan](https://x.com/TencentHunyuan/status/2125928146113585468) | ~30K views, 728 likes, 309 bookmarks.
- Verification: open weights verified (e.g. AngelSlim/DS-V4.1-Flash-DFly2-B6 on HF); 81% / 4.2× are author-claim.
- Take: Teams self-hosting DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash should try it this week; the gains are at 32K+ context.
