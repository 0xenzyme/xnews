### Naive RAG still wins: complex agentic memory isn’t always needed

- What happened: *Is Complex Agentic Memory Always Needed? Across LoCoMo / LongMemEval / MEME / MemoryAgentBench settings, Raw-RAG ranks first in six of seven compared setups, beating the strongest agentic-memory baseline by ~0.13–5.53 points. Authors argue higher evidence coverage can offset lower utilization; fancy mutation/structure isn’t always necessary, and naive RAG should be a required baseline.
- Why it matters: Same “delete complexity” direction as Anthropic’s fold-subagents guidance — aimed at the memory subsystem.
