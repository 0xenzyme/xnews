## 1. Models

### Google ships Gemini 4 Hydrogen, the fastest Gemini 4 tier, live in Cursor on day one

- What happened: After Carbon and Argon, Google released Gemini 4 Hydrogen for everyday tasks, coding, and agentic workflows. Sundar Pichai says it finishes tasks 2.5x faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash and has 4 thinking levels so you only wait and pay for what you need; DeepMind says it beats Argon on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. Logan Kilpatrick lists AI Studio (all tiers), Gemini API, Gemini for business, the Gemini app, Google Search, and Antigravity for paid AI Pro / Ultra. Cursor added it the same day, reporting 54.3% on CursorBench at default effort vs 39.6% for 3.8 Flash.
- Why it matters: This is the high-volume Gemini 4 tier; speed and price decide which model sits in the default agent loop.
- Source & engagement: [GoogleDeepMind](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2120940338869055788), ~740K impressions, 5,492 likes; [OfficialLoganK](https://x.com/OfficialLoganK/status/2120940951292268640), ~588K, 8,339 likes; [sundarpichai](https://x.com/sundarpichai/status/2120940236805169182), ~358K, 6,342 likes; [cursor_ai](https://x.com/cursor_ai/status/2120944639633211619), ~109K, 1,612 likes.
- Verification: Launch and availability verified (official posts). 2.5x speed and AA index are Google's own numbers, author-claim; CursorBench is Cursor's own, author-claim. The "$1.50 per million input tokens" and "5x fewer fabrications" figures in X's news summary don't appear in those official posts, unverified.
- Take: If your agent pipeline runs on 3.8 Flash, A/B it today on your own tasks before trusting leaderboards.

### Thinking Machines releases Inkling 2 Small weights under Apache 2.0

- What happened: @thinkymachines says Inkling 2 Small weights are on Hugging Face under Apache 2.0; Mira Murati says they shipped "as promised" after safety-testing partners reviewed it. Researcher Songlin Yang lists architecture changes: attention residuals (with a q/k/v split), gated attention, dynamic attention sink, dynamic short conv, value norm, and more.
- Why it matters: A frontier lab's large MoE under a genuinely permissive license is a counterexample to the "open weights have stalled" debate from 11/12.
- Source & engagement: [thinkymachines](https://x.com/thinkymachines/status/2120982625582195011), ~134K impressions, 1,162 likes; [miramurati](https://x.com/miramurati/status/2120983826407268458), ~51K, 1,348 likes; [SonglinYang4](https://x.com/SonglinYang4/status/2120988641376026691), ~14K.

- Take: Worth pulling for self-hosting and fine-tuning teams; check your memory budget first.

## 2. Agent platforms

### Self-hosted Claude Managed Agents (private beta); Gemini API Managed Agents get bring-your-own sandbox

- What happened: @ClaudeDevs announced self-hosted Claude Managed Agents in private beta: host the agent harness, sandboxes, and session history in your own cloud or on-prem, and choose where Claude requests go. Anthropic platform lead Katelyn Lesse says companies can now host it themselves. A day earlier Google AI Studio let Managed Agents on the Gemini API use self-hosted sandboxes, and Fly.io and other sandbox vendors followed.
- Why it matters: Both labs are handing "where the agent runs" back to the customer: planning stays in the cloud, execution and data stay on the customer side. That's a precondition for agents in regulated industries.
- Source & engagement: [ClaudeDevs](https://x.com/ClaudeDevs/status/2120938988165091693), ~478K impressions, 8,262 likes, 4,527 bookmarks; [katelyn_lesse](https://x.com/katelyn_lesse/status/2120968799130386656), ~6.4K; [GoogleAIStudio](https://x.com/GoogleAIStudio/status/2120610219235660183), ~71K, 760 likes; [flydotio](https://x.com/flydotio/status/2120635073192235101).
- Verification: Both launches verified (official posts). X's summary lists E2B, Modal, Vercel, Daytona, and Cloudflare as partners; only Fly.io was confirmed here, the rest unverified.
- Take: The high bookmark-to-like ratio says developers plan to use this. Teams with data-residency rules should apply.

### Claude Meetings: Claude joins your video call and builds docs and whiteboards while you talk
