## 1. Frontier models

### Anthropic releases Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, 20% cheaper

- What happened: @claudeai announced Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on Nov 9. Anthropic says they are the same underlying model: Fable carries strong misuse safeguards and is available on all paid plans, the Claude Platform and major clouds; Mythos is open only to vetted cybersecurity and biomedical organizations. Fable 5.2 costs $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, down from $10 and $50. Pre-release showcases: 1,000 Fable 5.2 agents fully formalized the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in 30 hours (Opus 5.5 reached 26% in 18 hours with the same setup); on 203 open MIPLIB problems it beat the listed best solution in 72 and proved optimality in 45; it designed three working electronic devices from a one-paragraph prompt; it led on the RoboDojo two-arm robot benchmark; Mythos 5.2 accurately modeled 28 of 34 gene-therapy capsids; two theoretical-physics preprints. Anthropic still recommends Opus 5.5 as the default for everyday coding and knowledge work.
- Why it matters: It is the first public "same model, two safeguard tiers" product line, with high-risk capability gated behind vetting, and the flagship got cheaper, which matters for heavy agent users.
- Source & engagement: [claudeai launch](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2119867114450268322), ~7.27M views, 69,928 likes, 6,567 reposts, 10,871 bookmarks; [pricing](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2119867138987176141), ~530K views; [Fermat formalization](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2119867129751056485), ~540K views, 4,466 likes; [electronics demo](https://x.com/claudeai/status/2119899459761131615), ~690K views, 12,994 likes; [AnthropicAI](https://x.com/AnthropicAI/status/2119867707546071125), ~500K views.
- Verification: Launch, pricing and availability are verified (official accounts plus the [Anthropic newsroom](https://www.anthropic.com/news)). Fermat, MIPLIB, capsid and physics results are author-claim; MIPLIB solutions were submitted for outside checking, and the chemistry routes have not been lab-tested (Anthropic's own words). The "30% faster output" figure in news summaries does not appear in the official posts: unverified.
- Take: Safe to write "Fable 5.2 is out, 20% cheaper." Attribute Fermat and MIPLIB results to Anthropic.

### Meta launches Muse Ember, a small model for everyday agent work

- What happened: @AIatMeta introduced Muse Ember on Nov 9 as its smallest and most efficient model, built for agent work: carrying workflows across apps, calling tools in sequence, running tests and making targeted code fixes. It has a 1M-token context and native text, image, audio and video input. It is on Meta's API and, the same day, on Google Cloud alongside Muse Wonder and Muse Spark 1.3.
- Why it matters: Small-model competition is shifting from price per token to total cost per task, which is what sub-agents, routing and bulk tool calls care about.

## 2. Chinese open source

### Meituan opens LongCat-2.5 weights: 1.6T total, ~48B active, MIT license

- What happened: @Meituan_LongCat released LongCat-2.5 weights on Nov 9 (the model launched Nov 2 as API-only). It is a natively multimodal MoE with 1.6T total and ~48B active parameters, aimed at long-horizon agent tasks, visual understanding and agentic coding (large codebases, full-project builds, design, 3D). The [Hugging Face model card](https://huggingface.co/meituan-longcat/LongCat-2.5) says training ran on domestic Chinese AI accelerators and ships a W4A4 quantized variant with ~43% higher decode throughput than W8A8. Self-reported scores include Terminal Bench v2.1 88.4, BrowseComp 91.9 and MCP Atlas 83.1, roughly level with GLM-5.3, kimi-k3 and DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash.
- Why it matters: Another trillion-scale Chinese open model, under a looser license (MIT) than most open-weight releases, with a practical domestic-chip plus INT4 deployment path.
- Source & engagement: [Meituan_LongCat](https://x.com/Meituan_LongCat/status/2119793531393040462), ~35K views, 954 likes, 110 reposts, 170 bookmarks; Nov 2 [launch post](https://x.com/Meituan_LongCat/status/2117264311714537633), ~325K views.
- Verification: Weights and license verified (official post plus Hugging Face). Benchmarks are Meituan's own or cited; the card says unstarred scores are indicative only (author-claim). The model page lists 1.8T stored parameters versus the advertised 1.6T, unexplained.
- Take: Write "Meituan open-sources a 1.6T multimodal agent model under MIT"; avoid "beats closed model X."

## 3. Safety infrastructure

### OpenAI details Control Room, which halts research jobs within 35 seconds

- What happened: @OpenAI described Control Room on Nov 9: a central system that tracks every research inference workload (including agentic sampling for training and evals, and data processing), works with its misalignment monitors, and halts model output within 35 seconds of a decision to stop or pause a job.
- Why it matters: Frontier labs are starting to publish how they stop agents running inside their own clusters, which fits right after this weekend's METR discussion about AI-accelerated R&D.
- Source & engagement: [OpenAI](https://x.com/OpenAI/status/2119913172039868579), ~280K views, 2,219 likes, 367 replies, 570 bookmarks; [blog](https://openai.com/index/control-room/).

- Take: Frame it as a published safety system, not as evidence of a past runaway incident.
