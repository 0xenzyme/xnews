## 2. AI for math and science

### Anthropic posts a Claude-authored VP ≠ VNP proof: 70-page paper, ~73K lines of Lean, no human review yet

- What happened: On Nov 23 Anthropic added a vp-vnp folder to [anthropics/formal-math](https://github.com/anthropics/formal-math/) with the paper "VP ≠ VNP over every field" and a Lean 4 formalization; the only author is Claude. The README says an internal research Claude model found it: over every field, circuits for the Hamiltonian-cycle polynomial (and, in characteristic ≠ 2, the permanent) need more than m^{c (log m)^{1/3}} gates for large m — a superpolynomial lower bound. The README says no human mathematician has reviewed it; secondary reports say CI also checks it with the independent nanoda kernel. There was no announcement.
- Why it matters: VP vs VNP is Valiant's 1979 "algebraic P vs NP." If it holds, it is the biggest lower bound in algebraic complexity in decades, but it does not settle P vs NP. It lands three days after the Riemann formalization; per @AndrewCurran_, it is also the second paper (after one on the Exponential Time Hypothesis) uploaded unannounced with an "internal research Claude model" as sole author.

### DeepMind's Gemini 4 agent teams find cheaper quantum circuits, all proved in Lean and open-sourced

- What happened: On Nov 23 Google DeepMind and Google Quantum AI published a [blog post](https://deepmind.google/blog/finding-faster-quantum-algorithms-with-agents-and-formal-verification/) and paper. Independent Gemini 4 agent teams improved known quantum algorithms; every idea had to be proved in Lean before entering a shared notebook, and teams met at a "virtual conference" every four hours. Results: an inertial-confinement-fusion circuit 11.8× cheaper (5,650 → 4,338 error-corrected qubits); a photochemistry simulation 8× cheaper; Cytochrome P450 2.5× with 481 → 333 qubits; a Grover implementation 2.57× faster; the common arcsin routine 290× faster. The Lean library, problem specs and solutions are on GitHub.
- Why it matters: Another template for "agents + formal verification" science: machines search, proofs gate, humans pick problems. The viral "up to 290×" refers only to the arcsin subroutine; DeepMind's own post says simulation costs fell "by up to 10 times."
- Source & engagement: [@GoogleDeepMind](https://x.com/GoogleDeepMind/status/2124911073266413634) | ~210K views, 3,367 likes; [@PolymarketMoney "up to 290x"](https://x.com/PolymarketMoney/status/2124917801471074497) | ~857K views, 19.6K likes.

## 3. Models and open source

### Zhipu GLM-5.4: first Loop Transformer flagship, 750B params, 28 layers run twice

- What happened: On Nov 23 Zhipu (Z.ai) released [GLM-5.4](https://z.ai/blog/glm-5.4), converted from the pretrained GLM-5 base by continued training: 28 weight-shared layers run for two iterations with no added parameters, which Z.ai says matches 2T–3T-parameter models. Three engineering changes: per-token gated progressive updates (to stop activation norms blowing up), shared cross-loop KV plus a second-pass sliding window (an extra loop costs only one window of KV, keeping 1M-context deployment affordable), and reuse of sparse-attention indices. In a 30B iso-parameter ablation, the looped model used ~10% more training compute and gained most on math, science and long context, with 10–14% shorter outputs. Positioned for "generalized coding" (video, games, 3D shaders, CAD) with new vision. It is live for GLM Coding Plan users with 50% more weekly quota than GLM-5.3. API access waits on safety evaluation; weights "will be released responsibly." Z.ai admits it can fall into overly long "marathon thinking."
- Why it matters: After Thinking Machines' Inkling 2 Mini, another lab takes looped transformers to flagship scale — and by retrofitting an existing base rather than training from scratch, which others can copy.
- Source & engagement: [@Zai_org](https://x.com/Zai_org/status/2124811995912286352) | ~873K views, 10.8K likes, 2,221 bookmarks; [@ZixuanLi_ Coding Plan rollout](https://x.com/ZixuanLi_/status/2124719275864670681) | ~1.1M views, 8,935 likes.

- Take: Coding Plan only for now; no API or weights. Speed is a real cost; Z.ai suggests GLM-5.3-Flash for simple tasks.
