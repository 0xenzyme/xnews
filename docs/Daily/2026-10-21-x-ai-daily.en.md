### ElevenLabs launches Eleven Realtime (speech-to-speech)

- What happened: @ElevenLabs shipped Eleven Realtime end-to-end S2S, claiming ~0.5s time-to-first-speech with spoken reasoning. Follow-ups cite v1 Max ~98.8% on Big Bench Audio / ~0.57s start, plus a cheaper Mini.
- Why it matters: Voice agents long traded speed vs accuracy. Unified S2S changes stack choices vs ASR→LLM→TTS for support, tutoring, and calls.
- Source & engagement: [Official](https://x.com/ElevenLabs/status/2112550782063247521) — ~172k views, 2,151 likes, 160 reposts; [TestingCatalog](https://x.com/testingcatalog/status/2112778083816140857) — ~3.0k views, 26 likes.
- Verification: verified — official product post; benchmark numbers should be checked against ElevenLabs/docs, not only X copy.
- Take: Measure interrupt handling, tool use, and multilingual latency yourself before rewriting your voice stack.
